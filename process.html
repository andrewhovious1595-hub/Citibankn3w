Process & standards

Professional visuals. Transparent foundations.

A repeatable workflow designed to keep source material, assumptions, and visual interpretation distinguishable at every stage.

Scope the question

Identify the audience, disputed issues, deliverables, deadlines, and intended use.

Inventory the record

Organize photographs, video, reports, plans, measurements, statements, and source metadata.

Build the visual model

Develop diagrams, annotations, timelines, perspectives, and comparisons tied to the available inputs.

Review & deliver

Check labels, scale, source references, assumptions, limitations, and revision notes before delivery.

Evidence integrity

Separate what is known from what is modeled.

Every project should make the status of its inputs legible. We distinguish source facts from estimates, assumptions, illustrative elements, and open questions.

  • Source register and provenance notes
  • Measurement references and scale disclosures
  • Assumption and limitation notes
  • Version history and review comments
  • Clear identification of demonstrative material

Clear Fault Legal provides visualization and litigation-support services. We do not imply that a demonstrative is itself an expert opinion, a scientific reconstruction, or admissible evidence. Expert analysis, technical conclusions, and testimony should be supplied or reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals.
