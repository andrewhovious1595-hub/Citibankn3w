Transportation incidents

Make the sequence, geometry, and damage understandable.

Visual analysis support for passenger vehicle, commercial truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, roadway, and multi-vehicle incidents.

01 / Scene & path analysis

Reconstruct the scene in context.

We create coordinated overhead and perspective exhibits that explain roadway layout, vehicle positions, approach paths, points of contact, and final rest locations.

  • Intersection and lane geometry
  • Vehicle position and orientation
  • Approach, travel, and departure paths
  • Road markings, signs, signals, and sightlines
02 / Damage & contact

Show the physical details.

Close-up annotations and comparative views can help communicate visible deformation, scrape patterns, contact areas, and damage relationships when supported by source photographs and inspections.

  • Damage-location maps
  • Component and contact-point callouts
  • Vehicle-to-vehicle comparison boards
  • Illustrative force and motion concepts clearly labeled as such
