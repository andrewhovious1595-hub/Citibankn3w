A disciplined process

Clear visuals start with clear foundations.

We begin by defining the question, inventorying source materials, and identifying what is known, inferred, assumed, or still unknown. Visual polish never substitutes for evidentiary support.

  • Documented source materials and provenance
  • Measurements and coordinate references where supplied
  • Explicit labels for estimates and assumptions
  • Review-ready drafts with revision tracking
